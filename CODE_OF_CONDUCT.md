# Code of Conduct
## Diversity and Inclusion

We are committed to making this project a welcoming and harassment-free space
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
We attach importance to diverse perspectives and will treat all contributors with respect.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including our GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, and reviews), team meetings, and team communication channels.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

1. Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
2. Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
3. Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
4. Using welcoming and inclusive language
5. Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

1. Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, whether in public or private
2. Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour
3. Sharing others' private information without their explicit permission
4. Bullying, intimidation, discrimination, or subtle expressions of bias
5. Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate
   in a professional setting

## Consequences

Depending on how serious the behaviour is, the project team may take one or more of the following actions:
1. Written Notice: A private, written message explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate and reminding the person of this Code of Conduct.
2. Meeting: A meeting to discuss the behaviour, after which the person agrees in writing to follow this Code of Conduct.
3. Report: If the behaviour continues, the team may contact the DSCI 522 teaching team and may remove the person from the project.

## Raising Concerns

If you experience or notice behaviour that goes against this Code of Conduct,
please let the project team know by mentioning any of us on GitHub:

- Eniola Oladimeji (@fifoooooo)
- Rachel Yan (@rachelyan419)
- Lareina Lyu (@lvifeng)
- David Zhang (@DHZhang123)

You may also reach out to the DSCI 522 teaching team instead.
We will respond within a few days and let you know how the concern will be handled.
If a concern involves a team member, that person will not take part in handling it. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith will not be tolerated.
