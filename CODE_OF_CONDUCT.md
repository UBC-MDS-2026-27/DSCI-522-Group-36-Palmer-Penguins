# Code of Conduct
## Our Pledge

We pledge to make this project a welcoming and harassment-free space
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
We attach importance to diverse perspectives and will treat all contributors with respect.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including our GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, and reviews), team meetings, and team communication channels.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, whether in public or private
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Sharing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Bullying, intimidation, discrimination, or subtle expressions of bias 
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
   
## Data acquisition

No manual download is needed. Rendering the Quarto report automatically downloads the raw dataset from the palmerpenguins repository into `data/raw/` and saves the cleaned dataset to `data/processed/`.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious the behaviour is, the project team may take one or more of the following actions:
1. **Correction:** For inappropriate or unprofessional behaviour, a private, written warning explaining why it was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** For a single serious incident or repeated behaviour, a warning with a period of no interaction with the people involved. Continuing the behaviour may lead to a ban.
3. **Temporary ban:** For serious or ongoing violations, a temporary ban from all interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward others, a permanent ban from the project.
